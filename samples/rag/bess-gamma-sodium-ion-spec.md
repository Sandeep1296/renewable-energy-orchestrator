# BESS-03 Gamma: Sodium-Ion Pilot Storage (proposed addition)

## Nameplate

- Chemistry: Sodium-Ion (Na-ion) prismatic cells
- Rated power: 25.0 MW continuous
- Nameplate capacity: 100.0 MWh (4-hour duration)
- Roundtrip AC efficiency: 84.5% at 0.5C

## Operating envelope (proposed)

- Recommended SOC range: 10.0% - 95.0%
- Emergency frequency reserve floor: 15.0% SOC
- Degradation factor: 0.00018% capacity fade per full equivalent cycle —
  between LFP (0.00015%) and NMC (0.00022%), so dispatch priority sits
  between BESS-01 Alpha and BESS-02 Beta.
- Thermal limits: nominal 15°C - 35°C; derate 25% above 45°C; trip above 55°C.

## Intended role

Absorb midday solar surplus that would otherwise be curtailed once Line South
approaches its 120 MW thermal ceiling, then discharge through the 18:00–21:00
evening ramp. Ideal for shallow daily cycling that would wear NMC packs.
